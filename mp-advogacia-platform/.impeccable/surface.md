# Home

Mode: Persuade, with informative legal copy and visitor-initiated contact.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An engraved architectural page tied to Brasilia, presenting the brand and three practice areas clearly.
OWN-WORLD: Royal blue ink, antique gold accents, near-white paper, Libre Baskerville display, Source Sans 3 body. Flat bands, discreet rules and architectural pillar tabs requested by the user.
STORY: Visitors identify the brand, region, lawyer and scope, select an area, resolve practical questions and initiate WhatsApp contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Large centered Marques Pimenta name above concise online-attendance text, gold WhatsApp button and visible area link, over an engraving with Brasilia architecture along the bottom, modernist columns at the edges, and an artistic interpretation of the seated Justice statue at the left. The statue was explicitly requested by the user. Mobile art sits below the text without overlap.
FORM: The engraved hero leads into a compact text introduction and responsible lawyer. Watermelon Tabs 1 informs a segmented three-area selector above a full-width paper reading panel. The building illustration and pillar gallery were removed at the user's request. FAQ uses a single generous reading column, followed by the editorial contact/footer close adapted from Watermelon Footer 12.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Design Read
ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2. The hero retains the architectural identity; subsequent sections prioritize practical content. Gold distinguishes contact actions and the selected tab's bottom accent. Pointer selection uses subtle color and opacity feedback; keyboard selection is immediate. Reduced motion disables transitions.
